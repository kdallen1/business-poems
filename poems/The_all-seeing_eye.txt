The all-seeing eye

A comet burns overhead the bed of
My pickup truck holds so much
Life yipping coyotes (they
turn human as daylight for a second)
a blanket you and me
And the all-seeing eye watches so much
Life slipping by
Seeing only her reflection cauterizes
those slipstreams
Into objects and moments
For her little menagerie

Now she has something
To put in the group chat
