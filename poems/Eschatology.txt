Eschatology

I am a man of utility
and faith
I desire nothing more than
to lie down and ripen in the dirt

Summon me to prayer
bang your
rain-soaked
semantron

Blessed are we who occupy
this great middle ground between ants and the sun
this garden of ingressing eyes
this slack maw speaking
the long word for truth

Blessed are the worms
who will not
become machine

They would rather
wriggle in topsoil
awaiting the plow
