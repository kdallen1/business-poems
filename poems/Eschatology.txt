Eschatology

I am a man of utility
and faith

To summon me to prayer
bang your rain-soaked semantron
I desire nothing more than to
lie down to ripen in
the dirt

Blessed are the worms
who cannot will not
become machine

Blessed are we who occupy this great
middle ground between ants and the sun
this garden of ingressing eyes
this sea-slack maw speaking
the long word for truth

We fractious children would rather
wriggle down in topsoil
awaiting the harrow
