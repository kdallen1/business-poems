Lapidary

Day in day out
Hot rock cool rock
Night sun eats light
Like glass then blood

Stones are worn by waves
Tide in tide out

I am digging something
It is not a grave
It is dark as one until
You let your eyes adjust

Look, you can see the stars
Touch slow carved stone
to your lips
Taste sea foam

Rough gems get
shaped in slow time

and sweet like muscadine grapes
or honeysuckle, one or the other
Something I ate wild off the vine
When I was young
