Stargazing

A comet flares overhead
the bed
of my truck holds so much
life, whole galaxies

Yipping coyotes (they
turn human as daylight
for a second), blankets, you and
me, all gazing stars

The all-seeing eye also
gazes, watches
stars from the bed
of my truck
glances over, are you
getting cold? Did you
make a wish?

Collecting in the comet's
slipstream just another moment
for her menagerie

Don't move
If we lie very still
she won't know we're alive
