Stargazing

A comet flares overhead
the bed of
my truck holds so much
life, whole galaxies,
yipping coyotes (they
turn human as daylight
for a second), blankets, you and
me, all gazing stars

The all-seeing eye also
gazes, watches
stars from the bed
of my truck
glances over, are you
getting cold? Did you
make a wish?

She gathers the comet's
slipstream
another moment for
her menagerie
