Stargazing

A comet flares overhead
the bed of
my truck holds so much
life, whole galaxies,
yipping coyotes (they
turn human as daylight
for one brief exposure
then return to the bubbling spring
of night), blankets, you and
me, all gazing stars

The all-seeing eye also
gazes, watches
stars from the bed
of my truck
glances over, are you
getting cold? Did you
make a wish?

She gathers up the comet's
slipstream: another
moment for
her menagerie
