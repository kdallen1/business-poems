Fired again

You said it’s not a good fit
You said you have to let me go
You can’t fire me, please, don’t
You remember me? Remember me

Standing as tall as humanly possible
on the highest mountain and you
the endless ocean where bathed
a little girl

We always talked about going
to the beach
but we never did. You swimming up and me
descending. Instead we laid
searching gazes through bright mornings
and I found the little girl always.
Swimming out to sea
