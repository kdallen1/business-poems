Fired again

You said it’s not a good fit
you have to let me go
I said you can’t fire me, please, don’t you
remember me? Remember me
Standing as tall as humanly possible
on the highest mountain and you
the endless ocean where bathed
a little girl

We always talked about going
to the beach (you swimming up
and me descending) but
we never did. Instead we laid
searching gazes through bright mornings where
I always found the little girl
Swimming out to sea
